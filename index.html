Cheesy Scrambled Eggs

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 3-4
Shredded Cheese Half a cup
Milk Quarter of a cup
Oil A tablespoon

Additional needs:

  • Frying pan
  • Whisk (or fork)
  • Stove
  • Bowl

Step 1: Set up the bowl

  • Wash your eggs over the sink before cracking them into the bowl
  • Mix the eggs until they are completely mixed
  • Pour the milk into the bowl
  • Mix the eggs and milk until it becomes a light yellow color
  • Put the cheese into the mixture
  • Mix until all the ingredients are together

Step 2: Set up the pan

  • Set your stove to a medium to high heat
  • Pour the oil into the pan and make sure that the oil has coated the whole pan

Step 3: The process

  • Pour your egg/cheese mixture into the pan. Make sure there is nothing in the bowl
  • Be near the pan for the entire experience
  • Wait for 4-5 minutes until you start seeing the edges becoming slightly brown
  • Using a spatula, start fliping the egg mixture
  • The egg mixture should start connecting more, getting out of the eggy mixture
  • Once it is scrambled or not liquidy anymore, turn off the stove

Step 4:Completition

  • Move the now scrambled eggs onto another plate and you are finished!
