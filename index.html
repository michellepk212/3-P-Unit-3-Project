Cheesy Scrambled Eggs

  • Time: 10 minutes
  • Serves: 1
Ingredients Quantity
Eggs 3-4
Shredded Cheese Half a cup
Milk Quarter of a cup
Oil A tablespoon

Additional needs:

  • Frying pan
  • Whisk (or fork)
  • Stove
  • Bowl

Step 1: Set up the bowl

  • Wash your eggs over the sink before cracking them into the bowl
  • Mix the eggs until they are completely mixed
  • Pour the milk into the bowl
  • Mix the eggs and milk until it becomes a light yellow color
  • Put the cheese into the mixture
  • Mix until all the ingredients are together

Step 2: Set up the pan

  • Set your stove to a medium to high heat
  • Pour the oil into the pan and make sure that the oil has coated the whole pan

Step 3: The process

  • Pour your egg/cheese mixture into the pan. Make sure there is nothing in the bowl
  • Be near the pan for the entire experience
  • Wait for 4-5 minutes until you start seeing the edges becoming slightly brown
  • Using a spatula, start fliping the egg mixture
  • The egg mixture should start connecting more, getting out of the eggy mixture
  • Once it is scrambled or not liquidy anymore, turn off the stove

Step 4:Completition

  • Move the now scrambled eggs onto another plate and you are finished!

Reheating Chicken Nuggets

  • Time:15 minutes
  • Serves: 5
Ingredients Quantity
Frozen Chicken Nuggets 15-20
Ketchup A tablespoon
Musturd A tablespoon

Additional needs:

  • Oven at 350 Farenheit
  • Tin Foil
  • Oven pan

Step 1: Set up your tin foil

  • Wrap your tin foil on an oven pan
  • Arrange your frozen chicken nuggets on the tin foil
  • Set your oven to a bake setting

Step 2: Waiting process

  • Wait for 15-20 minutes or when the chicken nuggets are golden brown
  • As you wait for the chicken nuggets to cool down, set up a plate with the cooling nuggets and ketchup and mustard
  • Enjoy!

Deli Meat Sandwich

  • Time:15 minutes
  • Serves: 1
Ingredients Quantity
Sourdough Bread 2 slices
Turkey or Ham 6-12 slices
Lettuce 2 leafs
Tomatos Half of a tomato
Pickles 8 slices
Mozzarella cheese 2 slices
Mustard Quarter of a cup

Additional needs:

  • Oven at toast setting
  • Butter knife
  • Oven pan

Step 1: Set up your tin foil

  • Wrap your tin foil around an overn pan
  • Place your bread onto the tray and toast it for 4 minutes

Step 2: Prepare your ingredients

  • Start by washing all your vegetables over cold water
  • Prepare your deli meat and cheese by slicing them eveningly
  • Once all the vegetables are washed, begin slicing them into equal pieces

Step 2: Setting up the sandwich

  • Take your bread out of the oven
  • Start by pouring mustard onto the bread and spreading it with a butter knife
  • Then place your deli meat and cheese
  • Place the vegetables on the bread on top of the deli meat and cheese
  • Fold your sandiwch together and slice in half
  • Enjoy!
